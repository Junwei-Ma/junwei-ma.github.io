abstract: >-
  This study investigates the spatial patterns and temporal variations in outage duration, intensity, and restoration/recovery following the 2024 Hurricane Beryl in Houston, Texas. This historic blackout 
  event caused widespread power disruptions across the Houston metropolitan area, leaving more than 2 million customers without power over several days, resulting in over 143 million total customer-outage hours.
