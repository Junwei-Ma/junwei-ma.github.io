pub:            "Sustainable Cities and Society"
pub_date:       "2024"
abstract: >-
  We combined fine-resolution flood damage claims data (composed of both insured and uninsured losses) and human mobility data (composed of millions of movement trajectories) 
  during the 2017 Hurricane Harvey in Harris County, Texas, to specify the extent to which vulnerability of the built environment (i.e., flood property damage) affects community recovery 
  (based on the speed of human mobility recovery) locally and regionally.
